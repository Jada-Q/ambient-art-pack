Ambient Art Pack

Six ambient art pieces, set as a Mac wallpaper or screensaver. The pieces rotate; their content updates live as they evolve.

Pieces

🌊 Tide Pixels

Ocean canvas. Sun, moon, tide direction.

🛬 Sky Traffic

Live aircraft trails over major airports.

🗼 Bay Ships

Vessels drifting through the world's bays.

🚇 Subway Pulse

Tokyo metro lines pulsing in scheduled time.

🪨 Quake Globe

Earth rotating with live seismic events.

🌙 Moon Phase

Tonight's moon phase + position over your city.

Install · 2 ways

Easy (double-click)

  1. Download latest .pkg
  2. Double-click the .pkg · macOS Installer opens · Continue · done
  3. First time: macOS Gatekeeper may block. Right-click the .pkg → Open → Open (one-time bypass)
  4. System Settings → Screen Saver → WebViewScreenSaver

One-line (developers)

brew tap Jada-Q/ambient-art-pack
brew install --cask ambient-art-screensaver

What it does

Installs WebView Screensaver (third-party, MIT-licensed) and points it at ambient-art-pack.vercel.app/list.json — a live list of 5 ambient art pieces that rotates every 10 minutes. Updates to the pieces (new content, new sprites, new pieces added to the list) appear automatically the next time your screensaver fires. No re-install needed.
